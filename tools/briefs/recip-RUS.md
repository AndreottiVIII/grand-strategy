## Albania (ALB) reads RUS as **rival**, stable, bond none, confidence C, since 2014

Note: Albania has adopted all EU Council decisions on Russia into domestic law since 2014 (decision 597/2025 lists over 2,500 persons and entities), turned back 207 Russian nationals at the border between 2022 and April 2026 and blocks RT and Sputnik; BIRN's July 2026 audit found almost no enforcement cases and calls trade and financial ties 'very limited' and relations cool for decades. Russia has no leverage on Tirana and does not recognise Kosovo, which Albania treats as kin. Rival, not adversary: Albania does not seek Moscow's defeat. Our reading of the structure (confidence C).
