## IDEA (at most three)

I1. **"Culminating point" is stretched to any cooling alliance** - `app.js:828`, `:836` (heading "Culminating points . allies cooling"), About
    `:1049`. In *Strategy* the culminating point is where an operation or a victory loses its force (the attacker's strength is used up, the
    victor's success summons the coalition). A cooling alliance or a warming rivalry is a trend, not a culminating point. The Russia and USA
    cards use the term properly (attrition war; coercion that wore itself out). Consider "Allies cooling" / "Rivals warming" as headings and
    keep "culminating point" for the paradoxes.
I2. **Two places where the atlas's own choices sit in his mouth** - `app.js:673` "the means ... across the four domains (diplomatic, informational,
    military, economic)" placed right after his levels: DIME is American doctrine, not his; and `:689` "'frozen' disputes are so prominent on these
    maps", given as the consequence of *Give War a Chance*: his argument is about interventions that stop wars early, the atlas does not model
    interventions. Say "the atlas's four domains" and drop "which is why".
I3. **The biography block on a living reader** - `app.js:668` ("he has not seen it"), `:682-683` (family background, his town of residence). He
    will read these about himself on a site he did not authorise. Consider cutting the residence and rewording the honour paragraph for the day
    after he has seen it.
