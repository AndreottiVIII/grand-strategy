## Bhutan (BTN) reads CHN as **rival**, stable, bond grievance, confidence C, since 1998

Note: No diplomatic relations, no treaty of friendship, an undelineated border (a 1961 Chinese map altered the traditional line), and Thimphu has an honorary consul in Macau and Hong Kong instead of an embassy. The 1998 agreement on peace and tranquillity in the border areas is the only settled text, and the 2021 roadmap and 2023 joint demarcation team are the current track. Doklam (2017) and the Chinese villages (22 by Barnett's count, eight near Doklam, reported by satellite in Dec 2024) are the friction. Bhutan keeps a disciplined silence and China denies building them. No source calls the relation rival: the grade is our reading of competing claims and frictions with no will to destroy the other (confidence C, per the Kosovo-Serbia precedent). Bond - grievance: the 1950 invasion of Tibet and the border that was never settled.

## Palau (PLW) reads CHN as **rival**, stable, bond grievance, confidence B, since 2020

Note: Rival, not adversary: Palau does not seek Beijing's disappearance; it refuses a price. In 2020 a Chinese ambassador to a neighbouring country pledged a million visitors if Palau capitulated on Taipei, an offer the president rejected (AP). In 2024 President Whipps said China was weaponising tourism to push Palau from Taipei; Chinese arrivals halved that year. Trend stable: the 2025 rebound (19,060 Chinese arrivals of 57,370, January to October, up 25%) is not read as a thaw, since no source explains it. Bond grievance: the coercion is an open wound in the president's own words. Confidence B: the government statement is tier 1 and was reported by AP (tier 3).
