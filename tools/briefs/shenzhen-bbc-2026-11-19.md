Title: "Trump, Putin and Xi release 'peace declaration' after historic meeting"
Publisher: BBC News. Authors: Harriet Whitehead and Jaroslav Lukiv. Published 19 November 2026 ("5 hours ago" at reading time). Tier 2.

Facts as the article states them:
- Trump, Putin and Xi issued a "joint declaration of peace" after 90 minutes of trilateral talks at the Kylin Villa in
  Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the APEC summit; their first trilateral meeting (they had met one-on-one before).
- The declaration says "nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought"; the three countries plan to increase the
  production and supply of petroleum; they are "committed to reform the UN"; it flags the immunity of state officials and
  says "the International Criminal Court and other similar institutions must not abuse their jurisdiction".
- Trump called the trip "a great and highly consequential trip" (social media post on Air Force One) and announced a
  White House news conference on Friday 20 November.
- Trump met Putin before the trilateral, their first meeting since August 2025, greeting him as his "friend"; he spoke
  separately with Xi, describing the relationship as "very special".
- Opening remarks: Xi said "positive interaction among major powers is not a matter of choice, but a mandatory
  requirement"; Putin cited a shared interest in ending the Ukraine war, food and energy security; Trump said the three
  are "uniquely positioned to discuss how to reduce the number of nuclear weapons on Earth", must "combat the threat of
  nuclear proliferation in other countries", "that's what we're doing in Iran", "we're not going to let Iran have a
  nuclear weapon"; he said he and Putin had a "long discussion" on Ukraine and that Putin "and I hope the other side,
  would like to see an end to that war".
- Putin's press conference: an "important chapter in the history of diplomacy"; Ukraine and Europe "not yet ready to make
  compromises"; Russia invited EU representatives to Moscow; he vowed a "far more painful" retaliation for the Ukrainian
  attacks on the Crimean bridge (a missile and drone attack on Tuesday 17 November destroyed its central road section);
  on Yalta comparisons: "Nobody divided up the world at Yalta... the realities on the ground will have to be accepted."
- While the talks were under way, Kyiv was under Russian drone attack.
- Xi, per CCTV: the meeting was "positive, constructive, and highly productive"; he recalled "the history of fighting
  side-by-side during WW2" and "cherishing the hard-won peace".
- The trilateral was reportedly Putin's idea; Russian papers called it a "diplomatic victory"; first international
  gathering with Western leaders (Canada, Japan, Australia present) Putin attended since the invasion of Ukraine.
- US reaction: Republican Senator John Curtis "encouraged" by the nuclear commitment but "Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping
  are not our friends".
- The article's own analysis headline: "Trilateral summit high on spectacle, short on substance".
