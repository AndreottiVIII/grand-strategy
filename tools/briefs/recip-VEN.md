## Panama (PAN) reads VEN as **rival**, warming, bond grievance, confidence C, since 2024-07-29

Note: Panama suspended diplomatic relations on 29 July 2024, the day after Venezuela's presidential vote that the opposition said Maduro had lost, and pulled its diplomats out of Caracas; Mulino said that silence would make Panama complicit.
