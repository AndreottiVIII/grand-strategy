## Afghanistan (AFG) reads USA as **rival**, stable, bond grievance, confidence B, since 2021-08-15

Note: No diplomatic relations, no embassy, no aid. Trump demanded Bagram back on 20 Sep 2025 ('bad things' otherwise); Fitrat cited the 2020 Doha Agreement and Mujahid said the Emirate will 'never' hand over any part of the country. USAID's Afghan programmes were cut in 2025 (SIGAR: USD 765m humanitarian plus about USD 1bn basic services). A prisoner channel (Boehler and Khalilzad in Kabul, September 2025) freed several Americans but, per Zelin, delivered nothing durable; Muttaqi called in August 2026 for a US embassy in Kabul and US investment, and the State Department refused and disowned Khalilzad's presence at the Saudi gas signing. Rival, not adversary: Washington does not seek the regime's end, and Kabul asks for an embassy. Bond - grievance: the 2001-2021 war, Bagram, the frozen reserves.

Rival and not adversary: the US does not seek the end of the state. Trend warming on the junta's side (it courts), but the US has yet to move. Bond grievance.

## North Korea (PRK) reads USA as **rival**, warming, bond grievance, confidence B, since 1950-06-25

Note: Rival, not adversary - argued. Pyongyang's stated aim is acceptance of its arsenal and an end to 'hostile policy', not the end of the American state; Kim told the SPA on 21 Sep 2025 that he has 'a good memory' of Trump and that there is 'no reason not to sit down' if Washington drops the 'absurd obsession with denuclearising', and the Ninth Congress repeated that the two could 'get along' on that condition. What would make it adversary is the regime-change fear, and Kim cites it (US action in Venezuela, per analysts) as the reason the arsenal is permanent; but no document sets the American state's defeat as an aim. Warming from the Trump outreach (Trump on 19 Aug 2026: he will meet Kim 'later this year', Kim Yo Jong denies any contact); not warm enough to be a partner: the sanctions (OFAC designations of 12 Mar 2026), the ICBM and enrichment disclosures and the Bybit heist all point the other way. Hanoi legacy: the second summit collapsed on 28 Feb 2019 and Kim suspended nearly all cooperation with the South afterward (AP). Bond - grievance: the Korean War and 'hostile policy'.
