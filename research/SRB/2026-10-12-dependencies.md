# Serbia - material dependencies research (raw, 2026-10-12)

Raw report from the research agent (about 163k tokens). Directed Serbia -> counterpart, each with number, unit, year, source. Curated version: the dependency rows in data/relations/SRB.yaml.

## 1. Natural gas -> Russia
- ~75% of annual gas consumption covered by Srbijagas's Gazprom contract (Energy Community annual report 2025, cited by AFP). Price under current annex EUR 318/1,000 m3 vs ~EUR 868 market (Vučić). - AFP via Connaissance des Énergies, 30 Sep 2026, https://www.connaissancedesenergies.org/afp/la-serbie-obtient-une-prolongation-de-trois-mois-de-son-accord-gazier-avec-la-russie-260930 (tier 3; underlying Energy Community).
- Contract status: 2022 three-year contract (2.2 bcm/yr) expired May 2025; Moscow refused a multi-year deal; quarterly annexes since. Latest: 3-month extension to 31 Dec 2026, same terms (Srbijagas CEO Bajatović on RTS). - N1, 30 Sep 2026, https://n1info.rs/english/news/srbijagas-ceo-bajatovic-gas-supply-deal-with-russia-extended-through-end-of-year/ (tier 3). bne IntelliNews: Russia's share ~75% in 2025 vs >90% a year earlier, https://www.intellinews.com/serbia-secures-three-month-extension-of-russian-gas-deal-434444/ (tier 3). Winter 2025/26 Gazprom volume 10 mcm/day to end-March 2026 - Tanjug, 25 Dec 2025, https://www.tanjug.rs/english/economy/217849/.
- Azerbaijan: contract up to 400 mcm/yr 2024-2026; ~300 mcm cumulative delivered Jan 2024-Oct 2025 (Babayev, Belgrade, 23 Oct 2025) - APA, https://en.apa.az/finance/azerbaijan-exports-300-million-cubic-meters-of-gas-to-serbia-since-january-481653 (tier 3). Bulgaria-Serbia interconnector 1.8 bcm/yr, operational Dec 2023 (S&P Global, https://www.spglobal.com/commodity-insights/en/news-research/latest-news/natural-gas/051225-serbia-eyes-favorable-conditions-for-future-russian-gas-imports-vucic).

## 2. Oil -> Russia (NIS) / Croatia (JANAF)
- Ownership: Gazprom Neft 44.85% + 11.3% via Gazprom-controlled vehicle (=56.15% Russian), Serbian state 29.87%. - OSW, 21 Oct 2025, https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-10-21/us-sanctions-against-serbian-oil-company-nis (tier 2).
