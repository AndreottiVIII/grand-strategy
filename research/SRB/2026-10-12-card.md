## 4. Paradoxes (Luttwak)

1. Kosovo veto requires Russia/China, which blocks the EU path. Serbia withholds alignment precisely to keep Moscow/Beijing's UN cover on Kosovo (EWB/ISAC 2026, T3), while the Commission states the "overall pace of negotiations will continue to depend on... normalisation of Serbia's relations with Kosovo" (EC 2025 Report, T1); von der Leyen demanded sanctions alignment for membership (Oct 2025; torre.news, T4 - verify).
2. "Neutrality" financed and armed by the West. Neutral Serbia's flagship purchase is French (Rafale), its growth model rests on 58% EU trade and its ammunition fed Ukraine.
3. Hedging buys leverage but no trust. The IIPE (Belgrade) volume argues the "Four Pillars" are "ad hoc, reactive alignment management" and "increasingly unsustainable". - https://doi.fil.bg.ac.rs/pdf/eb_book/2025/iipe_rsrfgnplcy/iipe_rsrfgnplcy-2025-ch1.pdf | IIPE | 2025 | T2. ECFR: "Under Vucic, Serbia is not playing - and will not be able to play - the partnership role"; urges threat of frozen funds and EXPO withdrawal. - https://ecfr.eu/article/after-novi-sad-how-the-eu-can-help-serbia-resurrect-its-democratic-path | ECFR | 31 Oct 2025 | T2. After Moscow, Vučić admitted the atmosphere for opening chapters was "not all that good". - https://n1info.rs/english/news/vucic-i-am-aware-many-in-eu-disapprove-of-my-visit-to-moscow/ | N1 | 13 May 2025 | T3.
4. Russian energy "bargain" became a liability (OSW 2026, T2).
5. Lithium leverage cuts both ways. SWP: EU interest in Jadar "weakens its ability to insist on reforms"; domestic protest forced Rio Tinto to mothball the project (Nov 2025). - https://www.swp-berlin.org/en/publication/the-eus-raw-materials-diplomacy-serbia-as-a-test-case | SWP | 12 Mar 2025 | T2; https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-13/rio-tinto-mothballs-flagship-jadar-lithium-project-in-serbia | Bloomberg | 13 Nov 2025 | T3.
6. "Colour revolution" narrative imports Russian framing that the EC cites against Serbia's "strategic direction" (EC 2025 Report, T1).
7. EXPO 2027 as showcase and hostage: cited by Vučić as a reason to end protest "turbulence" (BIRN 2026), by ECFR as a lever the EU could withdraw.
